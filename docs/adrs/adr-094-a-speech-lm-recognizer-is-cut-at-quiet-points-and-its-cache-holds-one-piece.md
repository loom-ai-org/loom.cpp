# ADR-094: A Speech-LM Recognizer Is Cut at Quiet Points, and Its Cache Holds One Piece

Serves [Epic-03](../epics/epic-03-model-coverage.md) (family 3, composition ASR); the second long-form
scheme beside [ADR-063](adr-063-a-long-clip-is-decoded-the-way-its-reference-decodes-it.md)'s. Code:
`include/loom/core/asr_long_form.h` (`QuietSplitPolicy`, `plan_quiet_splits`, `piece_token_budget`),
`src/core/transcribe.cpp` (family-3 branch), `loom-exporter/loom_exporter/oruk_loop_export.py`.

## Context

oruk-loop-153M is Qwen3-ASR-1.7B distilled into two looped stacks (one shared block per stack, a
low-rank adapter per depth). It exports through family 3's template, and it was trained and evaluated
on pieces of at most 30 s: its own `transcribe` cuts longer audio at the quietest 100 ms frame in the
last 5 s before each 30 s limit, decodes every piece alone with `min(448, ceil(10 s) + 24)` tokens, and
joins the pieces' texts with spaces.

`transcribe` did none of that for family 3. A file declaring a chunk (`loom.samples_per_chunk`) got one
pass over the whole clip, so the template sized every such export's KV cache at 4096 positions -- for
this model 28 layers x 2048 floats x 4096 = **940 MB at F32 beside a 243 MB Q8_0 file**. ADR-063's
scheme did not apply: it is NeMo's overlapping windows with a token-level LCS merge, and this reference
neither overlaps nor merges.

## Options

1. **Keep the one pass and the 4096 cache.** A 95 s clip runs, but past 30 s the model is outside what
   it was trained on, and the cache costs four times the weights.
2. **Reuse ADR-063's windows with this model's numbers.** A long transcript would differ from the
   reference's at every seam, each difference needing its own argument -- the reason ADR-063 rejected
   a home-grown scheme for Canary.
3. **Chunk in loom-py.** `loom_cli` would not get it, and `transcribe` owns long-form decoding for that
   reason.
4. **Port the reference's split into the engine, with its numbers declared by the file, and size the
   cache to one piece.**

## Decision

Option 4. A file declares `loom.asr.split_max_samples`, `split_search_samples`, `split_frame_samples`,
`split_min_samples` and the per-piece budget `loom.asr.tokens_per_second` / `tokens_base` /
`tokens_cap`. `transcribe`'s family-3 branch, when they are declared, cuts the waveform with
`plan_quiet_splits` (the reference's `split_points` decision for decision: the first of equally quiet
frames wins, as `np.argmin` returns it; a remainder of exactly the limit is one piece), drops pieces
shorter than the minimum, decodes each piece copied out on its own (so what follows it in the caller's
audio is not what pads its last chunk), passes the budget as `max_new_tokens`, and joins the stripped
texts with single spaces. Undeclared, nothing changes.

The export reads the window numbers off the checkpoint's own `split_points` signature; the four inside
`transcribe`'s body are restated there with the line they come from. The cache is then **one piece**:
390 audio rows + 18 prompt positions + the 448-token cap = 856, rounded up to 1024 -- 235 MB.

## Consequences

* Measured (loom.cpp main + this, 4 threads, x86): on 95 s of LibriSpeech, five pieces at the
  reference's own cuts, the F32 transcript is the reference's chunked `transcribe` exactly (WER 8.27%);
  Q8_0 is one word apart (7.87%). Peak RSS 1.94 GB / 963 MB. On 73 single clips F32 matches the
  checkpoint 73/73.
* **Needs an engine with this (1.0.0-rc20).** An older one ignores the keys and decodes one pass, which
  the 1024-cell cache holds for about 55 s; past that it stops with `this step needs N KV cells but the
  cache holds 1024 -- raise the model's declared 'loom.kv_cache_size'`, not a wrong transcript. The card
  builder derives the "Needs rc20" note from the key (`FEATURE_FIRST_RUNTIME`).
* Stock Qwen3-ASR and Granite declare nothing and are byte-identical (Qwen3-ASR-0.6B Q8_0, `cmp` against
  main). A family-3 leaf whose reference chunks this way now declares four numbers rather than paying
  for a 4096 cache.
* Pinned by `tests/ci/test_asr_long_form.cpp`: ten cases generated by the reference's `split_points`
  (a dropped 1500-sample tail and an all-zero tie among them) and the budget at 30 s, 0.85 s and 60 s.
  Making the tie `<=` fails it.
* The quiet-frame energy is accumulated in double where the reference uses float32; only an exact tie
  in float32 that is not one in double could cut differently, by one frame.
